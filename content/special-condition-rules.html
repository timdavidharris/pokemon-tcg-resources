SPECIAL CONDITIONS

Official status rules and checkup sequence reference.

☠️ POISONED
1st IN CHECKUP
When a Pokémon is Poisoned, put a Poison marker on it.

During Pokémon Checkup, place 1 damage counter on your Poisoned Pokémon.

A Pokémon cannot have two Poison markers; a new Poison condition simply replaces the old one.
Special Condition PTCG Rule
🔥 BURNED
2nd IN CHECKUP
When a Pokémon is Burned, put a Burn marker on it.

During Pokémon Checkup, place 2 damage counters on your Burned Pokémon, then flip a coin. If heads, it recovers (remove marker).

A new Burn condition replaces the old one.
Special Condition PTCG Rule
💤 ASLEEP
3rd IN CHECKUP
Rotate card counterclockwise.

Asleep Pokémon cannot attack or retreat.

During Pokémon Checkup, flip a coin. Heads = recovers (rotate right-side up). Tails = stays Asleep.
Special Condition PTCG Rule
⚡ PARALYZED
4th IN CHECKUP
Rotate card clockwise.

Paralyzed Pokémon cannot attack or retreat.

Recovers automatically during Pokémon Checkup after its owner's next turn.
Special Condition PTCG Rule
💫 CONFUSED
ATTACK CHECK
Rotate card upside down.

You must flip a coin before attacking. Heads = attack works normally. Tails = attack fails, and place 3 damage counters on your Confused Pokémon.
Special Condition PTCG Rule
✨ RECOVERING
OVERLAP RULES
Moving to the Bench or evolving clears ALL Special Conditions.

Since Asleep, Confused, and Paralyzed all rotate the card, whichever happened last overrides previous orientation conditions.

Poisoned & Burned use markers, so a Pokémon can be Burned, Paralyzed, and Poisoned all at once!
Core Mechanics PTCG Rule
📋 POKÉMON CHECKUP
CHECKUP SEQUENCE
Pokémon Checkup is a brief phase that happens between turns (after your turn ends, but before your opponent's turn begins). During Checkup, resolve effects in this order:
  1. Special Conditions: Resolve Poisoned, Burned, Asleep, and Paralyzed in that strict order.
  2. Abilities & Trainer Effects: Apply any Abilities, Trainer cards, or card text that state they happen during Checkup/between turns.
  3. Knockouts: Check if any Pokémon reached 0 HP from Checkup damage, resolve Knockouts, and promote new Active Pokémon before the next player starts their turn.
Note: The player whose turn is next decides the order of non-Condition abilities/effects, but all Special Conditions must resolve as one uninterrupted block!
