TURN ACTIONS

Official step-by-step guide for what you can do on your turn.

DRAW A CARD
You must do this at the beginning of every turn.
BENCH BASIC POKÉMON
Put Basic Pokémon onto your Bench (as many as you want).
ATTACH ENERGY
Attach 1 Energy card to 1 of your Pokémon (only once per turn).
RETREAT ACTIVE POKÉMON
Retreat your Active Pokémon (only once per turn).
EVOLVE POKÉMON
Evolve Pokémon (as many as you want).
PLAY TRAINER CARDS
Play Trainer cards (as many as you want, but only one Supporter card per turn).
USE ABILITIES
Follow the directions on the card.
ATTACK
Attack with your Active Pokémon. If you cannot attack, tell your opponent your turn is over.

Now it's your opponent's turn.
