Turn Actions Quick Reference

Standard sequence and permitted actions during your turn

DRAW CARD
Mandatory step at start of turn before taking any other actions.
ATTACH ENERGY
Max 1 Energy card per turn from hand to 1 Pokémon.
PLAY SUPPORTER
Max 1 per turn (player going first cannot play one on turn 1).
PLAY STADIUM
Max 1 per turn (cannot play a Stadium with the same name).
RETREAT POKÉMON
Pay Retreat Cost to switch Active Pokémon with a Benched one.
