SPECIAL CONDITIONS

Official status rules and checkup sequence reference.

☠️ POISONED
1st IN CHECKUP
If your Pokémon is Poisoned, put a Poison marker on it.

During Pokémon Checkup, put a damage counter on your Poisoned Pokémon.

A Pokémon cannot have two Poison markers—if it gets Poisoned again, the new Special Condition replaces the old one.
Special Condition PTCG Rule
🔥 BURNED
2nd IN CHECKUP
If your Pokémon is Burned, put a Burn marker on it.

During Pokémon Checkup, put 2 damage counters on your Burned Pokémon, then flip a coin. If heads, the Pokémon recovers.

A Pokémon cannot have two Burn markers—if it gets Burned again, the new Special Condition replaces the old one.
Special Condition PTCG Rule
💤 ASLEEP
3rd IN CHECKUP
If your Pokémon is Asleep, turn it counterclockwise.

A Pokémon who's Asleep can't attack or retreat.

During Pokémon Checkup, flip a coin. If heads, the Pokémon recovers.
Special Condition PTCG Rule
⚡ PARALYZED
4th IN CHECKUP
If your Pokémon is Paralyzed, turn it clockwise.

A Paralyzed Pokémon can't attack or retreat.

Your Pokémon will remain Paralyzed throughout your next turn and then recover during Pokémon Checkup.
Special Condition PTCG Rule
💫 CONFUSED
ATTACK CHECK
If your Pokémon is Confused, turn it so the top of the card points toward you.

Before attacking with a Confused Pokémon, flip a coin. If tails, the attack doesn't happen, and you put 3 damage counters on your Confused Pokémon. (If heads, the attack works normally.)
Special Condition PTCG Rule
✨ RECOVERING
OVERLAP RULES
Special Conditions are removed by moving the Pokémon to the Bench or evolving it.

Asleep, Confused, and Paralyzed: only the newest one applies — your Pokémon can't be Asleep and Confused at the same time.

Burned and Poisoned apply at the same time as other conditions, so a Pokémon could be Burned, Paralyzed, and Poisoned all at once!
Core Mechanics PTCG Rule
📋 POKÉMON CHECKUP
CHECKUP SEQUENCE
Pokémon Checkup is a brief phase that happens between turns (after your turn ends, but before your opponent's turn begins). During Checkup, resolve effects in this order:
  1. Special Conditions: Resolve Poisoned, Burned, Asleep, and Paralyzed in that strict order.
  2. Abilities & Trainer Effects: Apply any Abilities, Trainer cards, or card text that state they happen during Checkup/between turns.
  3. Knockouts: Check if any Pokémon reached 0 HP from Checkup damage, resolve Knockouts, and promote new Active Pokémon before the next player starts their turn.
Note: The player whose turn is next decides the order of non-Condition abilities/effects, but all Special Conditions must resolve as one uninterrupted block!
Turn Structure PTCG Rule
