TURN ACTIONS

Checklist for actions restricted to 1 per turn.

DRAW CARD
Mandatory action taken at the start of your turn.
ATTACH ENERGY
Attach 1 Energy card from hand to 1 Pokémon.
PLAY SUPPORTER
Max 1 per turn (player going first cannot play one on turn 1).
PLAY STADIUM
Max 1 per turn (cannot play a Stadium with the same name).
RETREAT POKÉMON
Pay Retreat Cost to switch Active Pokémon with a Benched one.
