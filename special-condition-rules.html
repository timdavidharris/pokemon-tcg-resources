Special Conditions Reference

Quick rules overview for status conditions during battle

💤 ASLEEP
ROTATE 90° LEFT
Rotate card counterclockwise.

Cannot attack or retreat.

Between turns, flip a coin: Heads = Pokémon wakes up (remove condition). Tails = Pokémon stays Asleep.
Special Condition PTCG Rule
🔥 BURNED
MARKER REQUIRED
Place a Burn marker on card.

Between turns, put 2 damage counters (20 damage) on your Burned Pokémon.

Then flip a coin: Heads = Burn condition is cured (remove marker).
Special Condition PTCG Rule
⚡ PARALYZED
ROTATE 90° RIGHT
Rotate card clockwise.

Cannot attack or retreat.

Removed automatically at the end of your next turn.
Special Condition PTCG Rule
☠️ POISONED
MARKER REQUIRED
Place a Poison marker on card.

Between turns, put 1 damage counter (10 damage) on your Poisoned Pokémon.
Special Condition PTCG Rule
💫 CONFUSED
ROTATE 180° UPSIDE DOWN
Rotate card upside down.

You must flip a coin before attacking. Heads = attack works normally. Tails = attack fails, and place 3 damage counters on your Confused Pokémon.
Special Condition PTCG Rule
✨ GENERAL RULES
OVERLAP RULES
Moving to the Bench or evolving clears ALL Special Conditions.

Since Asleep, Confused, and Paralyzed all rotate the card, whichever happened last overrides previous orientation conditions.

Poisoned & Burned use markers, so a Pokémon can be Burned, Paralyzed, and Poisoned all at once!
Core Mechanics PTCG Rule
